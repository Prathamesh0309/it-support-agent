VPN TROUBLESHOOTING
===================

Issue: VPN keeps disconnecting
--------------------------------
Symptoms: VPN drops every 10-30 minutes, especially on Wi-Fi.

Steps to fix:
1. Go to Network Settings → VPN → Advanced
2. Set MTU size to 1300
3. Disable power saving on your network adapter
4. Switch to 5GHz Wi-Fi or use a wired connection
5. Reinstall VPN client from it-portal.company.com if issue persists

---

Issue: Cannot connect to VPN at all
-------------------------------------
1. Check your internet works without VPN first
2. Use your company email as username (not personal)
3. Enter the 6-digit MFA code within 30 seconds
4. After 5 failed attempts, contact it-support@company.com
5. Try from a mobile hotspot to rule out firewall issues

---

PASSWORD & ACCOUNT ISSUES
==========================

Issue: Forgot or expired password
-----------------------------------
- Passwords expire every 90 days
- Self-service reset: accounts.company.com/reset
- Verify via mobile OTP
- New password rules: min 12 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special character

Issue: Account locked out
--------------------------
- Locks after 5 failed login attempts
- Wait 15 minutes for auto-unlock OR
- Contact IT: it-support@company.com or Slack #it-help
- Urgent unlock: call ext. 4357
